BODY · SOUL · SPIRIT · CALLING

Living for what lasts.

What has God uniquely entrusted to each of us—and what would it look like to live for an impact that extends beyond our lifetime?

A dedicated conversation

We envision a relaxed, optional 90-minute gathering during our time in Nosara, with space for reflection, meaningful questions, encouragement, and prayer. Date and time to be decided together.

Questions to bring

  • What gifts, passions and opportunities has God entrusted to me?
  • Where do I feel most alive and most useful to others?
  • What am I building that could outlast me?
  • How do my family, work, resources and relationships serve my calling?
  • What is one step of faith or obedience I sense God inviting me into?

Before we arrive

Optional preparation: take 15 minutes to reflect on a moment when you sensed purpose and alignment with God's calling. Bring a story, question, or dream—not a polished presentation.

This is an invitation to explore, not an obligation to share personal experiences.
