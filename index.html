☀️ Weather During Our Trip

We are heading to Nosara as Costa Rica’s Pacific coast transitions into its dry season. Expect warm beach weather, plenty of sunshine, and the possibility of passing showers in early December. This is a seasonal outlook, not a daily forecast.

🌡️ Typical December temperatures

Average highs around 84°F (29°C) and lows around 73°F (23°C), based on historical Nosara climate averages.

🌦️ Sunshine & showers

December is the beginning of the Pacific dry season, although early-month rain is still possible. Keep plans flexible for outdoor excursions.

🎒 What to pack

Light breathable clothes, swimwear, sun protection, a reusable water bottle, sandals or walking shoes, and a lightweight rain layer.

Getting around & trip logistics

✈️ Airport transfer

Liberia (LIR) to Nosara / Playa Pelada: approximately 2¼–3 hours by road, depending on traffic and conditions. Pre-arrange a private shuttle or rental vehicle. Plan to arrive before dark.

Local transport guide ↗

🚙 Local roads

Expect gravel and unpaved stretches. An SUV or 4WD can be useful, especially if exploring Ostional or driving after rain. Don't assume coastal shortcuts are passable.

👕 What to wear

No formal attire needed! Think comfortable, breathable, tropical casual.

Beach & pool: Swimwear, cover-up, sandals, hat and sunglasses.
Restaurants & sunset dinners: Casual shorts, sundresses, linen shirts or a relaxed button-down; sandals are fine at most spots.
Adventures & trails: Activewear, sturdy walking shoes or secure sandals, and clothes you don’t mind getting muddy.
Morning movement & yoga: Comfortable workout clothes.
Evenings: A light layer and optional rain jacket.

For a special dinner or speakeasy, feel free to dress up a little if you enjoy it—but absolutely no suits, ties, or formal dresses required.
