📍 Simple Signal check-in rhythm

Send a quick update at these milestones so we know everyone is safe and on schedule. A passenger should send messages, never the driver.

Arriving in Costa Rica → Nosara

  1. Landed: airport and actual arrival time.
  2. Ready to depart airport: after immigration, baggage, and meeting your ride; share ETA.
  3. On the road: quick update around the Nicoya area or another planned rest stop, when safe.
  4. Reached Nosara: send a check-in and updated ETA to the villa.
  5. At Casa Catalina: final “arrived safely” message.

Returning from Nosara → Airport

  1. Leaving villa: departure time and airport ETA.
  2. Mid-route: update around Nicoya or a planned stop, when safe.
  3. At airport: arrived and preparing to check in.
  4. Through security / boarding: brief update if practical.
  5. Home safely: final check-in after travel.

The route and timing depend on the airport, road conditions and driver. Download offline Google Maps, save the villa's Plus Code X85G+CF6, and avoid relying on Signal when cellular data is unavailable. For a real emergency in Costa Rica, call 911.

☀️ Weather During Our Trip

We are heading to Nosara as Costa Rica’s Pacific coast transitions into its dry season. Expect warm beach weather, plenty of sunshine, and the possibility of passing showers in early December. This is a seasonal outlook, not a daily forecast.

🌡️ Typical December temperatures

Average highs around 84°F (29°C) and lows around 73°F (23°C), based on historical Nosara climate averages.

🌦️ Sunshine & showers

December is the beginning of the Pacific dry season, although early-month rain is still possible. Keep plans flexible for outdoor excursions.

🎒 What to pack

Light breathable clothes, swimwear, sun protection, a reusable water bottle, sandals or walking shoes, and a lightweight rain layer.

Before leaving, save both the Costa Rica 40 main website and the Emergency & Safety Guide as PDF files on your phone. These are offline backups; check the live website for updates.

Save both pages offline before flying: iPhone (Safari): Share → Print → pinch open the preview → Share → Save to Files. Android (Chrome): ⋮ → Share → Print → Save as PDF. Repeat for the main website and Emergency Guide, then test both files in airplane mode.

iPhone (Safari): save each page as a PDF
  1. Open the page in Safari and tap Share (square with an up arrow).
  2. Choose Print. On the print preview, spread two fingers apart to open the preview as a PDF.
  3. Tap Share again → Save to Files → On My iPhone → Save.
  4. Repeat for the other page, then open both PDFs in Files to confirm they work without internet.

On some iOS versions, Share → Options → PDF → Save to Files is available instead.

Android (Chrome): save each page as a PDF
  1. Open the page in Chrome. Tap the three-dot menu → Share → Print (wording varies by phone).
  2. Select Save as PDF as the printer, then tap the PDF/download icon and save to Downloads or Files.
  3. Repeat for the other page, then open both saved files in airplane mode to confirm offline access.

👕 What to wear

No formal attire needed! Think comfortable, breathable, tropical casual.

Beach & pool: Swimwear, cover-up, sandals, hat and sunglasses.
Restaurants & sunset dinners: Casual shorts, sundresses, linen shirts or a relaxed button-down; sandals are fine at most spots.
Adventures & trails: Activewear, sturdy walking shoes or secure sandals, and clothes you don’t mind getting muddy.
Morning movement & yoga: Comfortable workout clothes.
Evenings: A light layer and optional rain jacket.

For a special dinner or speakeasy, feel free to dress up a little if you enjoy it—but absolutely no suits, ties, or formal dresses required.
