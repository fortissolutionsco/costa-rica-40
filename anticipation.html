Why anticipation matters

Click the play button below to hear Arthur C. Brooks explain why looking forward to a vacation is part of enjoying it. The video starts at his anticipation segment (15:24).

Put the anticipation into practice:
  1. Know your why: Choose what you most want from Costa Rica—rest, connection, adventure, growth, or presence.
  2. Explore before we arrive: Browse the villa, learn about Nosara, and choose one experience you're excited about.
  3. Build excitement together: Share an idea or a photo with the crew and start a conversation now.
  4. Keep expectations realistic: Leave room for surprises, unstructured time, and imperfect moments.
  5. Be present when we're there: Capture a few memories, but don't spend the trip behind a screen.
